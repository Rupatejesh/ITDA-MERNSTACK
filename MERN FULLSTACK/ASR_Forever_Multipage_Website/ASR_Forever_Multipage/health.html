Disaster Preparedness & Tribal Health

Heavy rainfall, landslides, road blockages and long distances from remote habitations can make emergency access difficult. Prepared communities and reliable health and transport systems save lives.

🌧️

Landslide Preparedness

Early-warning awareness, safe routes and monsoon emergency kits.

🚑

Emergency Transport

108 ambulance and feeder transport coordination in hill hamlets.

🤰

Maternal Care

Timely antenatal checkups, Birth Waiting Homes and safe delivery.

🏥

Health Network

PHCs, Village Health Clinics and Sickle Cell screening awareness.

🩸

Blood Availability

Youth blood donor network and hospital blood-bank coordination.

🛣️

Remote Road Access

Documenting culvert and road connectivity issues for action.

🚁

Future Drone Solutions

Regulated air-mobility concepts for hard-to-reach areas.

📡

Emergency Communication

Signal points, solar backups and community alert trees.

“Distance should never become a barrier between a tribal family and emergency care.”
