01 • OUR LAND • OUR RIGHT

Tribal Land Protection

Every tribal community should understand the protections applicable to Scheduled Area land, maintain important records and know where to seek lawful assistance when land alienation is suspected.

⚖️

Know the Law

Build awareness about the Fifth Schedule, applicable Scheduled Area land-transfer protections and official procedures.

📜

Know Your Records

Understand pattas, survey details, ownership documents and other official land records.

🛡️

Protect Lawfully

Document suspected violations and approach competent revenue, legal or official grievance authorities.

🧑‍🎓

Youth Awareness

Educated tribal youth can help elders understand documents, digital services and procedures.

“Know your land. Know your rights. Protect both with knowledge, courage and lawful action.”
