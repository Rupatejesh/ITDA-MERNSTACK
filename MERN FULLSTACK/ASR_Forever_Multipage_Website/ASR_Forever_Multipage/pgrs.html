PGRS Grievance Awareness

People should know how to clearly describe a public problem, preserve useful evidence, approach the appropriate authority and follow the official grievance process.

📷

Step 1: Document

Record relevant facts, dates, location and supporting photos.

📝

Step 2: Explain

Clearly state the problem, affected area and requested resolution.

🏢

Step 3: Correct Authority

Identify the exact department or official grievance channel.

📤

Step 4: Submit

Submit via official PGRS channels and save your reference ID.

🔎

Step 5: Track

Check official status and follow up on inspection remarks.

🤝

Youth Digital Support

Help villagers prepare clear submissions ethically.
