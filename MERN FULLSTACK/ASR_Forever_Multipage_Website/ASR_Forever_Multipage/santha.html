03 • OUR MARKET • OUR LIVELIHOOD

Tribal Santha & Markets

Weekly Santhas are important livelihood spaces for tribal farmers, forest-produce collectors, women, artisans and small vendors. Stronger infrastructure and transparent allocation strengthen the tribal economy.

⛺

Weekly Santha

Develop clean, organised and suitable spaces where eligible tribal farmers and vendors can sell their produce.

🏪

Local Tribal Shops

Encourage tribal youth and families to establish lawful, sustainable shops and services in their own communities.

🥬

Farmer to Customer

Support direct selling of vegetables, fruits, millets, coffee, turmeric, ginger, honey and other local produce.

⚖️

Fair & Lawful Markets

Suspected encroachments or rule violations should be documented and reported through competent authorities.

👩‍🌾

Women Vendors

Create stronger opportunities for tribal women, SHGs, farmers and small producers to participate in local markets.

📱

Digital Market

Youth can help local producers with digital catalogues, pricing information, branding and online promotion.

“Strong tribal producers + fair markets + local enterprise = stronger hill economies.”
