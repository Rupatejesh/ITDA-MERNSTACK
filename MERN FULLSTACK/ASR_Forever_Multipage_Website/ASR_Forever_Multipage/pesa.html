02 • OUR VILLAGE • OUR VOICE

PESA & Gram Sabha

Strong villages need informed community participation. Tribal communities should understand the role of Gram Sabha and the provisions applicable to Scheduled Areas.

🏡

Participate

Attend Gram Sabha meetings and understand the issues, proposals and decisions being discussed.

🗣️

Community Voice

Women, youth, farmers and elders should have meaningful opportunities to participate and express concerns.

📚

Understand PESA

Build awareness about the powers and responsibilities provided under applicable PESA provisions.

🤝

Community Participation

Village development becomes stronger when communities understand and participate in lawful local processes.

“An informed Gram Sabha strengthens community participation.”
